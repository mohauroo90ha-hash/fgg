Support that prevents problems instead of chasing them.

Technical Support at Globalinix keeps websites and systems healthy after launch. We handle updates, monitoring, backups, security hardening and bug fixes — the ongoing work that most businesses have no time for.

Support options can be tailored: light maintenance for a small site, or deeper monitoring and rapid-response cover for business-critical systems.
